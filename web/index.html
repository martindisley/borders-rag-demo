Civic Crossref

Borders Council Planning Doc Demo

This is a prototype implementation of Civic Crossref — a design pattern for helping the public scrutinise council policy by querying and cross-referencing planning and delivery document pairs. The documents queried here are hardcoded to two specific Scottish Borders Council publications.
